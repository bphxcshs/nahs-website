About NAHS

The National Junior Art Honor Society is a prestigious organization that recognizes students in grades 6-8 who have demonstrated outstanding ability and achievement in art.

At BASIS Phoenix, our NAHS chapter brings together talented young artists who share a passion for creativity and artistic growth. We provide opportunities for members to develop their skills, participate in community service projects, and showcase their work.

Our mission is to inspire and recognize students who have shown an outstanding ability and interest in art, fostering their continued involvement in the visual arts.

Membership Requirements

  • Be in grades 6-8 at BASIS Phoenix
  • Maintain a minimum GPA of 3.0
  • Demonstrate artistic ability through portfolio submission
  • Show a commitment to art and creativity
  • Complete an application during the open enrollment period

Applications open each fall semester. Stay tuned for announcements about the next enrollment period!
