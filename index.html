About NAHS

The National Art Honor Society at BASIS Phoenix brings together talented artists who share a passion for creativity, service, and leadership. We also support the National Junior Art Honor Society (NJAHS) for middle school artists.

Our mission is to foster artistic excellence, leadership, and a spirit of service among students. We are committed to cultivating creativity, inspiring collaboration, and encouraging the use of art as a means to engage, uplift, and positively impact our communities.

Through personal growth and collective effort, we strive to empower members to make meaningful contributions both within and beyond the classroom.

Accomplishments & Projects

🤝 Community Service & Outreach

Empty Bowls Project

Founded by President Tanvi, this was BASIS Phoenix's first Empty Bowls initiative, using student-created artwork to raise awareness about hunger.

The Memory Project

Created and sent original paintings to children in Armenia, fostering global connection, empathy, and hope through art.

Cards for Hospitalized Kids

Collaborated with the organization to design and send handmade cards to children receiving hospital care.

Million Thanks

Partnered with Million Thanks to create cards expressing gratitude and support for active military members.

Fireside Gratitude

Designed and delivered handmade cards to honor and thank local firefighters for their service.

Holiday Art Workshop (Senior Center)

Hosted a seasonal art workshop for senior residents, promoting creativity, connection, and community engagement.

Welcome to America Donation Drive

Donated $900 worth of art supplies to a local nonprofit supporting immigrant and refugee communities.

🎨 Public Art & School Initiatives

Collaborative Murals

Designed and painted a large-scale collaborative mural at the Festival of the Arts, with ongoing mural projects across the school campus.

School Murals (Ongoing)

Continuing to create permanent art installations that enhance shared spaces and reflect student voices.

Key Club Garden Benches

Designed and painted benches for the school's community garden in collaboration with Key Club.

Thanksgiving Gratitude Tree

Created a collaborative gratitude tree displayed in a school hallway to encourage reflection and appreciation.

🏆 Recognition & Exhibitions

National Recognition (NAEA)

Featured for two consecutive years in the National Art Education Association newsletter, highlighting both President Tanvi as a key student artistic leader and the society's community impact.

NAEA Student Advisory Council Representation

President Tanvi serves on the National Art Education Association Student Advisory Council, placing BASIS Phoenix National Art Honor Society among the top 12 art honor societies nationally.

Arizona Arts Council Representation

President Tanvi serves on the Arizona Arts Council Youth Advisory Council, allowing the society to gain insight into and contribute student perspectives to state-level arts discussions and initiatives.

Museum Exhibition

President Tanvi and Treasurer Willow were featured as exhibiting student artists in a museum exhibition.

The BASIS Phoenix National Art Honor Society is committed to student-powered service, artistic excellence, and meaningful community impact.

Membership Requirements

  • Be in grades 6-12 at BASIS Phoenix (NJAHS: 6-8, NAHS: 9-12)
  • Maintain a minimum GPA of 3.25
  • Take any art class in BASIS Phoenix
  • Show a commitment to art and creativity
  • Complete an application during the open enrollment period

Ready to Apply?

Applications open each year for both NAHS (high school) and NJAHS (middle school). Stay tuned for announcements about the next enrollment period!
